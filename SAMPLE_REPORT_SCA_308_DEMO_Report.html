ABOUT ADMINISTRATIVE SAFEGUARDS
===============================
Each 'Covered Entity' (Healthcare Delivery Organization) and
'Business Associate' (Contractor, Vendor, 3rd Party) must, in
accordance with §§ 164.306 (General Rules) and 164.316
(Policies, Procedures, and Documentation Requirements),
implement safeguards to protect the confidentiality,
integrity, and availability of all electronic protected health
information that it creates, receives, maintains, or
transmits.

NON-COMPLIANT AND PARTIAL COMPLIANCE | RECOMMENDATIONS AND GUIDANCE
============================================================
[ADM-02]
Standard: TECHNOLOGY ASSET INVENTORY
Specification: 164.308 (ii)(B) [Network Map]
Rule Source: A new standard proposed by the NPRM
Question: Has your organization developed a network map that illustrates the movement of electronic protected health information throughout your electronic information systems, including but not limited to, how electronic protected health enters and exits such information systems, and is accessed from outside of such information systems?
Answer: N | Finding: Non-compliant

Guidance: Create a written network map showing how ePHI moves through all your electronic information systems. Include all systems, connections, and data flows. Assign an owner and keep it current.

Technical: Develop a network map per CM-12 that illustrates all ePHI movement across relevant electronic information systems. Align with system component inventory per CM-8 and system security plan per PL-8.

Reference: NIST SP 800-53r5 | Configuration Management (CM) | Sections: CM-8, CM-12, PL-8 | Pages: 107, 112, 193

[ADM-03]
Standard: TECHNOLOGY ASSET INVENTORY
Specification: 164.308 (a)(1)(ii)(C)(1) [Maintenance]
Rule Source: A new standard proposed by the NPRM
Question: On an ongoing basis, but at least once every 12 months, does your organization review and update your written 'Technology Asset Inventory'?
Answer: P | Finding: Partial Compliance

Guidance: Establish a documented annual review cycle for your technology asset inventory. Assign responsibility for completing and recording the review each year.

Technical: Formalize the annual review process for the asset inventory per CM-8(2). Document review results and update the inventory to reflect current system state per CM-8(1).

Reference: NIST SP 800-53r5 | Configuration Management (CM) | Sections: CM-8(1), CM-8(2) | Pages: 108

[ADM-05]
Standard: RISK ANALYSIS
Specification: 164.308 (ii)(A)(1) [Assessment]
Rule Source: A REQUIRED standard in the current Security Rule but enhanced by the NPRM
Question: Does your organization perform or conduct a review of the technology asset inventory and the network map to identify where electronic protected health information may be created, received, maintained, or transmitted within your electronic information systems?
Answer: N | Finding: Non-compliant

Guidance: Review your technology asset inventory and network map to identify every location where ePHI is created, received, maintained, or transmitted. Document all findings as the foundation of your risk analysis.

Technical: Conduct a scope assessment per RA-3(1) using the system component inventory (CM-8) and network map to identify all ePHI locations as the basis for risk analysis.

Reference: NIST SP 800-53r5 | Risk Assessment (RA) | Sections: RA-3, RA-3(1) | Pages: 256, 257

[ADM-07]
Standard: RISK ANALYSIS
Specification: 164.308 (ii)(A)(3) [Assessment]
Rule Source: A REQUIRED standard in the current Security Rule but enhanced by the NPRM
Question: Does your organization identify potential vulnerabilities and predisposing conditions to your relevant electronic information systems?
Answer: U | Finding: Non-compliant

Guidance: Identify and document all potential vulnerabilities and predisposing conditions in your electronic information systems that could be exploited by identified threats.

Technical: Conduct and document vulnerability identification per RA-3 and RA-5 covering system weaknesses and predisposing conditions across all relevant electronic information systems.

Reference: NIST SP 800-53r5 | Risk Assessment (RA) | Sections: RA-3, RA-5 | Pages: 256, 259
